Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps

Circuit de Spa-Francorchamps is located in the Ardennes region of Belgium and is one of Formula 1's longest circuits.

The circuit combines long straights with fast corners and significant elevation changes.

Eau Rouge and Raidillon form one of its most famous sections, requiring a fast and precise sequence through an uphill combination of corners.

The circuit is also known for unpredictable weather because different parts of the track can experience different conditions.

Spa rewards a balance between straight-line speed and aerodynamic performance because the lap contains both high-speed sections and demanding corners.
