Marina Bay Street Circuit

The Marina Bay Street Circuit hosts the Singapore Grand Prix and is a street circuit located around the city of Singapore.

The Singapore Grand Prix became the first night race in Formula 1 in 2008.

The circuit is physically demanding because of its humid conditions, bumpy street surface and long race duration.

The track contains many corners and requires drivers to make frequent steering and braking inputs.

Singapore is different from traditional permanent circuits because the surrounding streets form the racing surface, leaving limited room for mistakes.
