Circuit of The Americas

Circuit of The Americas, commonly called COTA, is located in Austin, Texas.

Formula 1 first raced at the circuit in 2012.

The layout combines several different types of corners, including a sequence inspired by the characteristics of Silverstone and Suzuka.

The uphill approach to Turn 1 is one of the circuit's distinctive features and can create overtaking opportunities.

COTA therefore provides a mixture of high-speed corners, technical sections, elevation changes and long straights, requiring teams to find a compromise between aerodynamic performance and straight-line speed.
