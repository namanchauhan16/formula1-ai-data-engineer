Silverstone Circuit

Silverstone is a historic Formula 1 circuit located in Northamptonshire, England.

The circuit is known for its high-speed layout and a combination of fast corners, flowing sections, and technical parts.

Some of its famous sections include Maggotts, Becketts, Chapel, and Hangar Straight.

The British Grand Prix is held at Silverstone.

The circuit places significant demands on aerodynamic performance, tyre management, and driver precision, particularly through its fast corners.
