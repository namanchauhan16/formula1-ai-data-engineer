Suzuka Circuit

Suzuka Circuit is located in Suzuka, Japan, and has been part of the Formula 1 calendar since 1987.

The track has a distinctive crossover layout and is known for its fast and flowing corners.

The Esses, Degner curves and 130R are among its most recognizable sections.

Suzuka is particularly demanding because drivers need to maintain high speed while accurately positioning the car through sequences of corners.

The circuit rewards aerodynamic performance, precision and commitment. Its combination of high-speed corners and technical sections makes it an important test of both car and driver.
