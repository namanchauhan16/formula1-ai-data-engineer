Red Bull Ring

The Red Bull Ring is located in Spielberg, Austria, in the Styrian mountains.

The circuit has a relatively short lap but combines long straights, heavy braking zones and fast corners.

The first part of the lap places importance on power and acceleration, while the later sections contain quicker corners that require precise car control.

Elevation changes are an important characteristic of the circuit.

The track can create opportunities for overtaking because of its long straights followed by heavy braking zones.
