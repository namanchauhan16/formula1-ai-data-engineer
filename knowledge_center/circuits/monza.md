Autodromo Nazionale Monza

Monza is a Formula 1 circuit located near Monza, Italy.

It is commonly known as the "Temple of Speed" because of its very high-speed nature and long straights.

The circuit has relatively few corners compared with many other Formula 1 circuits and places strong importance on straight-line speed and braking performance.

The Italian Grand Prix is held at Monza.

Parabolica, now officially known as Alboreto, is one of the circuit's famous corners.
