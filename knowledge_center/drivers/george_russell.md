George Russell

George Russell is a British Formula 1 driver who progressed through the Mercedes junior programme before entering Formula 1 with Williams in 2019.

He won the GP3 championship in 2017 and the Formula 2 championship in 2018.

Russell scored his first Formula 1 podium with Williams in 2021.

He joined Mercedes as a full-time race driver in 2022 and achieved his first Formula 1 victory at the São Paulo Grand Prix that year.

Russell is known for strong qualifying performances, consistent race pace and an aggressive approach to overtaking.

He continues to race for Mercedes under the current Formula 1 regulations.
