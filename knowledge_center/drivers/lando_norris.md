Lando Norris

Lando Norris is a British Formula 1 driver who joined McLaren for the 2019 season.

Before Formula 1, Norris achieved success in junior single-seater championships and became part of McLaren's driver development programme.

He scored his first Formula 1 podium at the 2020 Austrian Grand Prix.

Norris secured his first Formula 1 victory at the 2024 Miami Grand Prix.

He continued to compete at the front with McLaren and became Formula 1 Drivers' World Champion in 2025.

Norris is known for strong qualifying pace, racecraft and an aggressive approach to racing.
