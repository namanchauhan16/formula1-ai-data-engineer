Oscar Piastri

Oscar Piastri is an Australian Formula 1 driver who joined McLaren for the 2023 season.

Before entering Formula 1, Piastri won the Formula Renault Eurocup, Formula 3 and Formula 2 championships.

He made an immediate impression during his rookie season by scoring multiple podium finishes.

In 2024, Piastri achieved his first Formula 1 victories and helped McLaren secure the Constructors' World Championship.

His driving is characterized by a calm approach combined with strong racecraft and consistent development through the junior categories.

Piastri remains a McLaren driver and continues to compete at the front of the Formula 1 field.
