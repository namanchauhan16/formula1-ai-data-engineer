Charles Leclerc

Charles Leclerc is a Monegasque Formula 1 driver who made his championship debut with Sauber in 2018.

Before Formula 1, Leclerc won the GP3 Series and Formula 2 championships.

Ferrari signed him for the 2019 season, where he quickly established himself as one of the team's leading drivers.

Leclerc achieved his first Formula 1 victory at the 2019 Belgian Grand Prix and followed it with his first Ferrari victory at the Italian Grand Prix one week later.

He is particularly known for strong qualifying speed and precise car control.

Leclerc has remained a Ferrari driver since joining the team in 2019.
