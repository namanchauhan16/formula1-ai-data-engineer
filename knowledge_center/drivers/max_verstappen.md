Max Verstappen

Max Verstappen is a Dutch Formula 1 driver who entered the championship at the age of 17.

He became the youngest driver to win a Formula 1 race when he won the 2016 Spanish Grand Prix after moving to Red Bull Racing during that season.

Verstappen won his first Drivers' World Championship in 2021 and followed it with further championships in 2022, 2023 and 2024.

His racing is known for strong wheel-to-wheel ability, aggressive racecraft and confident overtaking and defending.

Verstappen is the son of former Formula 1 driver Jos Verstappen and began competing at a very high level in karting before entering single-seater racing.
