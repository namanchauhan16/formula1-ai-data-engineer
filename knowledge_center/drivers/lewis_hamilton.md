Lewis Hamilton

Lewis Hamilton is a British Formula 1 driver who made his debut with McLaren in 2007.

He finished his rookie season as championship runner-up and won his first World Championship with McLaren in 2008.

Hamilton later moved to Mercedes, where he achieved multiple Drivers' World Championships and became one of the most successful drivers in Formula 1 history.

His career includes victories and podiums across many different generations of Formula 1 cars.

Hamilton is known for strong qualifying performances, race pace and his ability to perform in changing conditions.

He moved to Ferrari for the 2025 Formula 1 season.
